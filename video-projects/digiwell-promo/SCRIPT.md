# Digiwell Marketing — 30s Narration Script

**Voice:** mid-age male, calm confident delivery. Apple-keynote register. Silence between sentences is a feature, not dead air.
**TTS model:** Kokoro-82M, voice `am_adam`.
**Target duration:** 28–30s at natural pace (~2.5 words/sec, ~72 words total).

---

Your growth engine wasn't built for the A I era.

Cold leads. Dead lists. Campaigns that never convert.

Digiwell builds growth systems that do.

Forty seven percent open rates. Subscribers who actually buy.

Cold leads become loyal buyers. In thirty days.

Digiwell Marketing. Book your free audit at digiwell marketing dot com.

## Pronunciation notes

- **AI** → write as `A I` so TTS says each letter.
- **CRM** → write as `C R M`.
- **47%** → write as `forty seven percent`.
- **30 days** → write as `thirty days`.
- **digiwellmarketing.com** → write as `digiwell marketing dot com`.

## Tone checks

- Contractions used: "wasn't". ✓
- Sentence rhythm: medium, fragment-fragment-fragment, short, fragment-fragment-fragment, fragment + short, fragment + short, medium. Strong punch-list rhythm fitting a 30s promo.
- Opening line: bold claim that name-drops the "AI Era" positioning. Not generic. ✓
- Closing: explicit URL + "free audit" CTA (matches the site's own primary CTA). ✓
- Landed specifics in every beat: "AI-assisted newsletters, CRM automation, segmented nurture flows", "47% open rates", "30 days". No filler. ✓
