## Post body

The venture playbook is being rewritten at every stage.

Three signals from this week that all point the same direction:

→ Benchmark broke a 20-year tradition. After two decades capping funds at $425M, they just raised their first-ever growth fund: $1.25B of a $2B raise. The catalyst? Cerebras returning roughly 12x and proving that staying in beats cashing out early.

→ Menlo Ventures turned one Anthropic bet into the biggest raise in its 50-year history: $3B. That single position is now reportedly worth around $14B.

→ Seedcamp doubled its prior fund to $320M and is heading to the US.

Bigger first checks. Growth funds launching for the first time. And the firms making those calls are all pointing at the same thing: AI.

When a single conviction call reshapes an entire firm's strategy, it tells you exactly where we are in the cycle.
