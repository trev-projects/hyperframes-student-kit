# RVL — Physical AI — 30s Narration Script

**Design intent:** VO speaks OVER the visuals, adding context and analysis. The text on screen delivers the *facts* (PHYSICAL AI, $1.5B, deal names, thesis). The voice delivers the *take* (what it means, why now, what's next). They complement — never duplicate.

**Voice:** `af_heart` at 0.92× speed. Warm, confident, slightly conspiratorial — an insider giving you the read.
**Target duration:** 28–30s at natural pace.

---

## The script (clean, to send to TTS)

This week on R V L — the race to the physical world begins.

Software's era is ending. Atoms are taking over.

Physical A I is robotics meeting frontier models. Humanoids. Factories. Autonomy.

In the last ninety days, three rounds closed. Two point two billion dollars — just to get started.

Every major fund now has a dedicated pod. If yours doesn't, you're already late.

Software redefined value. Physical A I will redefine reality.

Full breakdown on R V L. Sign up at R V L dot tech.

## Beat overlay — VO lines speak OVER, not duplicate

| Beat                    | Visual on screen                      | VO line spoken OVER it                                               |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 0 · Teaser (0–3.2s)     | THIS WEEK → PHYSICAL AI → $1.5B       | "This week on RVL — the race to the physical world begins."          |
| 1 · Cold open (3.2–5.2) | PIXELS ARE OUT.                       | *(silent beat — lets visual punch)*                                  |
| 2 · Flip (5.2–7)        | ATOMS ARE IN.                         | "Software's era is ending. Atoms are taking over."                   |
| 3 · Category (7–14.2)   | PHYSICAL AI + cycling examples        | "Physical AI is robotics meeting frontier models. Humanoids. Factories. Autonomy." |
| 4 · Deal rail (14.2–22) | 3 deals count up                      | "In the last ninety days, three rounds closed. Two point two billion dollars — just to get started." |
| 5 · Thesis (22–26.2)    | EVERY TOP FUND / PHYSICAL AI THESIS   | "Every major fund now has a dedicated pod. If yours doesn't, you're already late." |
| 6 · Signoff (26.2–30.7) | SOFTWARE ATE / PHYSICAL AI WILL MOVE  | "Software redefined value. Physical AI will redefine reality."       |
| 7 · CTA (30.7–33.5)     | RVL + tagline + CTA line              | "Full breakdown on RVL. Sign up at rvl dot tech."                    |

The VO provides 7 short lines, each ~2–4 seconds. Gaps between lines = room for the visual to breathe and punch on its own.

## Pronunciation

- **AI** → `A I` (each letter)
- **RVL** → `R V L` (each letter)
- **rvl.tech** → `R V L dot tech`
- **1.5B / $1.5 billion** → `one point five billion`
- **2.2 billion** → `two point two billion`
