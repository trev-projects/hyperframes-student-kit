# Digiwell Trailer — Storyboard

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, **30s total** · **Audio:** music bed only, no VO · **Hook:** `GROWTH IS A SYSTEM.`

## Structure

Philosophy → Philosophy expanded → Services+Results fused → Philosophy bridge → CTA. The middle beat does double duty so the video doesn't need a separate "service list" + "stat rail" — they live together.

| # | Beat | Window | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Cold open | 0 – 4.5s   | `GROWTH IS / A SYSTEM.` over particle-trail |
| 1 | Thesis | 4.5 – 9.5s | `MOST AGENCIES POST. / WE ENGINEER GROWTH.` |
| 2 | The system | 9.5 – 18s  | 3 cards — service + outcome stat + tagline + connector arrows |
| 3 | Bridge | 18 – 22.5s | `When the system runs, / the numbers follow.` |
| 4 | CTA outro | 22.5 – 30s | Wordmark + URL + Free Audit pill, 5+ s hold |

## Beat 2 details — the centerpiece

Three glass cards in a row, each fusing service + icon + outcome stat:

| Card | Service | Icon | Outcome | Tagline |
|------|---------|------|---------|---------|
| A | AI NEWSLETTERS | envelope | 47% open rate | Warm the list every week. |
| B | CRM AUTOMATION | lightning bolt | 12× pipeline ROI | Score every lead in real time. |
| C | NURTURE FLOWS | branching tree | 30 days to lift | Convert at the right moment. |

Cards cascade in (0.5s stagger). Then **amber connector arrows draw between them** (`stroke-dashoffset` 1 → 0) — that's what makes it read as a SYSTEM not a list. Stat counters animate from 0 to final.
