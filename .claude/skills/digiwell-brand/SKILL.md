# Digiwell brand kit

1. **Read `brand/digiwell/README.md` in full before writing copy or code.** It holds positioning, copy
   guardrails, approved proof/credits, palette, type, wordmark, motion, components and assets.
2. **Reference build:** `video-projects/digiwell-sizzle/`. Scenes are generated by
   `scripts/_write_scenes.py` (shared shell: fonts, cedar ground, corner wordmark, grain, 9:16 detection);
   `scripts/build-roots.mjs` writes the 16:9 root and the 9:16 sibling project in `build/vertical/`.
   Copy its patterns rather than inventing new ones.
3. **Start a new piece** with the copy commands in README section 9. Use the kit's `tokens.css`,
   `brand.js` and `fonts/`; never pull fonts from Google at render time.
4. **This brand overrides `MOTION_PHILOSOPHY.md`'s look** (no chrome text, perspective grids or amber
   whips) but keep its discipline: one idea per beat, motion in transitions, breathing outro.
5. **Hard copy rules:** no em dashes, no AI-agency language, no guarantees, no invented stats or
   testimonials, no placeholders on screen. The Breakthrough Academy testimonial needs Trevor's
   confirmation before public use.
6. **OpenAI** (key injected by the proxy; send `Authorization: Bearer dummy`): `gpt-image-2` for
   grounds/textures/illustrations only (never type, logos, people or fake screenshots),
   `gpt-4o-mini-tts` for VO, `gpt-4o-transcribe` for captions. Save outputs into `brand/digiwell/assets/`.
7. **Verify** both formats by pulling a frame per scene and looking at it before handing anything over.
   With `<video>` in a scene, check that overlays and shades render on top of the footage.
8. **Phone footage is HDR (HLG).** Tonemap to Rec.709 SDR before use (README section 7 has the ffmpeg
   line); HDR sources make the renderer composite video over text and wash it out.
